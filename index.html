What is FX Rewards?

FX Rewards is a trading cashback platform that tracks eligible trading activity and applies rewards according to the applicable reward rate and terms.

How do I get started?

Sign up for free, open an eligible trading account, wait for verification, then trade eligible instruments.

How is my reward calculated?

Your estimated reward is based on trading volume multiplied by the displayed reward rate for the selected instrument category.

What is the current reward rate?

For the current website calculator, Forex & Gold use $7 per 1.00 lot and Indices use $75 per 1.00 lot.

Is FX Rewards a broker?

No. FX Rewards is not a broker and does not execute trades on behalf of users.

Does FX Rewards provide trading advice?

No. FX Rewards does not provide investment, trading or financial advice.
